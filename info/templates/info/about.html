About Emertat Farabar

International Logistics Built on Experience

Emertat Farabar is an international freight forwarding and transportation company providing road, rail, sea, multimodal transportation and customs clearance solutions from Iran to regional and international markets.

Our Company

Established in 2009. Backed by Decades of Logistics Experience.

Emertat Farabar was established in Iran in 2009 by logistics professionals with extensive previous experience in international transportation and freight forwarding.

Our management team brings more than 22 years of hands-on industry experience, combining practical knowledge of cross-border transportation, freight coordination and regional logistics.

Today, we support importers, exporters, manufacturers, trading companies and international logistics partners with transportation solutions designed around each shipment’s cargo, route, timing and operational requirements.
