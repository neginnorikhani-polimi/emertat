Integrated Logistics

When One Mode of Transport Is Not Enough

International cargo does not always move efficiently using a single transportation mode. Emertat Farabar coordinates multimodal logistics solutions that combine road, rail and sea freight according to the shipment route, cargo characteristics, timing and commercial requirements.

Transport Modes

Connecting Road, Rail & Sea Freight

Road Freight

Road transportation can support cross-border movements as well as first-mile and final-mile connections within a multimodal route.

Rail Freight

Rail transportation can support suitable long-distance and regional corridors and connect with road or maritime segments.

Sea Freight

FCL and LCL sea freight can be integrated with inland road or rail transportation through Iran's southern ports.

Common Combinations

Integrated Freight Routes

The most appropriate combination depends on the origin, destination, cargo and route. Our team evaluates each shipment individually before recommending a multimodal solution.

Sea + Road

Sea freight through Iranian ports combined with inland road transportation toward the final destination.

Rail + Road

Rail transportation combined with road freight for first-mile, final-mile or cross-border connections.

Sea + Rail + Road

Multiple transportation modes coordinated as part of a wider international or regional logistics route.
