FCL & LCL

Flexible Sea Freight Options

FCL

Full Container Load

Dedicated container capacity for larger shipments or cargo that benefits from being transported in a full container under one booking.

LCL

Less than Container Load

A practical option for smaller shipment volumes that do not require a full container, helping businesses optimize shipping costs where consolidation is suitable.
